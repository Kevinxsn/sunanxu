Experience

  • 2025 - present

    San Diego, CA

    De novo sequencing of long, highly charged peptides
    Advisor: Prof. Pavel Pevzner
    I combine mass spectrometry, algorithm design, and protein language models to reconstruct long peptide sequences.
    • I established a framework that integrates two-dimensional mass–mass spectrometry (MMS) fragment correlations into de novo sequencing to constrain candidate sequences and reconstruct long peptides.
    • I designed an approach to the antisymmetric-path problem that enforces consistent peak assignments and integrates MMS correlations for peptides that challenge conventional tandem mass spectrometry.
    • I am fine-tuning protein language models to recover missing residues in gapped MMS reconstructions by combining learned sequence priors with spectral evidence.

    Single-cell gene regulatory networks
    Advisor: Prof. Bing Ren
    I worked on transformer-based methods to infer gene regulatory networks and study the effects of regulatory variation.

    • I designed and implemented single-cell benchmarks to evaluate regulatory interactions across cellular contexts, with a focus on predictive robustness and interpretability.

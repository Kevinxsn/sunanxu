I am a Ph.D. student in [**Bioinformatics**](https://bioinformatics.ucsd.edu) at [**UC San Diego**](https://www.ucsd.edu), advised by [**Prof. Pavel Pevzner**](https://jacobsschool.ucsd.edu/faculty/profile?id=119) in the Department of **Computer Science and Engineering**. My research focuses on developing algorithms for de novo sequencing of long, highly charged peptides. I combine two-dimensional mass–mass spectrometry with algorithm design to reconstruct sequences that are difficult to resolve with conventional approaches.

I also completed a research rotation with [**Prof. Tiffany Amariuta**](https://amariutalab.org), focusing on statistical genomics. During this rotation, I worked on fine-mapping-based methods to estimate disease heritability without using individual-level patient data.

I earned my B.S. at [**UC San Diego**](https://www.ucsd.edu) in June 2025, graduating _cum laude_ with a double major in Data Science through the [**Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute**](https://datascience.ucsd.edu) and Bioinformatics through the [**School of Biological Sciences**](https://biology.ucsd.edu).

During my undergraduate studies, I worked with [**Prof. Bing Ren**](https://www.nygenome.org/about-us/our-people/senior-leadership/bing-ren-phd/) on single-cell gene regulatory networks, integrating multiple omics data types to contribute to an atlas of the mouse brain. I also developed methods for evaluating large language models with [**Prof. Jingbo Shang**](https://shangjingbo1226.github.io). Across these projects, I am interested in combining biological knowledge, statistical reasoning, and machine learning to make predictions more reliable and interpretable.

Alongside research, I enjoy helping students build intuition for algorithms, data science, and biology through [teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}).
